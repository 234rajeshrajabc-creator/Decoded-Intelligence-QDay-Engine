Methodology

Quantum readiness is treated as a dependency-chain problem rather than an algorithm replacement exercise.

Quantum Readiness = min(Crypto, PKI, HSM, Certificate, Static Data, Vendor, Interoperability, Archive)

The engine is a scenario and decision-support framework. It does not predict the date of Q-Day.
